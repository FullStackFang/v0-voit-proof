## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reasoning is shown beside each applicant
Each genuine applicant SHALL show their reasoning, one line per call in their own words, beside the gold score. Reasoning SHALL never change the order. Tokens issued before reasoning existed SHALL show without it.

#### Scenario: Same score, different reasoning
- **WHEN** two genuine tokens both score 3 of 3 with different reasoning
- **THEN** they stay one tier in paste order, each with its reasoning shown
