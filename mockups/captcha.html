A gate, not a page.

The challenge stops living as a big tile in the middle of the form. It behaves like a captcha: one small row in the form, the calls open in a window over the page, and the form cannot be sent without it. Two ways to do it below. The calls themselves look the same as mockup 03, just tighter.

A · Checkbox gateA small row in the form. Click it, the window opens, finish, the box ticks. Submit is grey until then.
B · On submitNothing in the form but a marked submit button. Clicking it opens the window; finishing sends the application.

My pick is A: the candidate sees the three minutes before filling in the form, not as a surprise at the end.
