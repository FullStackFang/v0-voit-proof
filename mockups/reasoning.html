Not just the answer. How you got there.

Inside the voitProof window, as built (centred). The candidate first picks the skills they are strongest in. Then each call is three quick taps: Spot (tap the lines you would check first), Call (what you do), Why (what decided it). The answer is one data point; the order you looked in and the reason you gave are the workflow, which is the part worth collecting.

Three calls of three taps keeps it near three minutes, so the separate written note goes: the Why step is the written part, and it is shorter.

05 · What gets collected

Per call: skill, lines in order with timing, the call, the reason. Stored per answer as today, and signed into the result.

The employer reads it in /verify and /pool as a short trace. The score still comes only from the call, so ordering stays deterministic; Spot and Why are the data.
