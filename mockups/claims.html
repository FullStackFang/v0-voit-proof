-- Average grade per course, for the term report
SELECT c.title, AVG(g.score)
  FROM courses c
  LEFT JOIN grades g ON g.course_id = c.id
 WHERE g.term = '2026-autumn'
 GROUP BY c.title;

Courses with no grades yet are missing from the report. Why?

AAVG skips nulls, so those rows are dropped.
BThe WHERE on g.term turns the LEFT JOIN into an inner join.
CGROUP BY needs c.id as well as c.title.
